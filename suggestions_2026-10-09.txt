[RED] BUSINESS / DATA NA BUG (pehla karva jeva)
------------------------------------------------
1. GST hamesha 9% CGST + 9% SGST
   - Bija state no client/supplier hoy to IGST 18% aavvo joie.
   - Product ma taxPercent chhe pan Add Sale / Add Purchase form e vaaprtu nathi.
   - Asar: invoice ma tax khoto aavse.

2. Stock fakt whole number ma
   - "CF Bulk" Kilogram ma chhe, pan 12.5 kg vechi/kharidi shakata nathi
     kemke stock column Int chhe.
   - Fix: stock decimal karvo (DB migration + ledger + forms).

3. Invoice number nu prefix code ma fixed chhe
   - PO-2024 / SO-2024 hardcoded chhe; 2026 ma pan 2024 lakhay chhe.
   - Fix: financial year pramane (PO/26-27/0001) server par number banavvo,
     jethi be jan ek sathe save kare to number na takraay.

4. Mobile Add Purchase / Add Sale ma "Order Paid" field j nathi
   - Mobile thi banelo order hamesha unpaid gane chhe.
   - Asar: Dues, Amount Paid card ane Transactions entry khota thay.

5. Purchase list nu "Items" column judi judi units no sarvalo kare chhe
   - Example: 100 pcs + 200 kg = "300" dekhay.
   - Fix: lines ni sankhya batavvi (e.g. "2 items").

6. Delivery sweep server na timezone par chale chhe
   - Render jeva UTC server par order 5:30 kalak moda Received/Delivered thase.
   - Sweep thi Received thay tyare "Received On" ma expected date nahi pan
     sweep chalyu e time aave chhe.
